\begin{resumo}[Abstract]			% Título do Resumo (Abstract = Resumo em inglês)
\begin{otherlanguage*}{english}		% Língua do texto
Currently, flaws in the calculation of sentence execution have contributed to the undue retention of convicts in the Brazilian prison system, worsening a scenario already marked by an increase in the prison population. Among the factors involved, one of the most recurrent stands out: errors in the Criminal Execution Process (PEC). The PEC is the set of procedures that accompanies the serving of the sentence, including calculations, granting of benefits, and registration of work and/or study actions for sentence reduction, according to the Penal Execution Law (LEP). When performed manually, this calculation is susceptible to errors that go unnoticed, which can result in longer prison periods than legally foreseen or in undue early releases, affecting the management of prison spaces and public safety. The correct serving of the sentence and the effective calculation, considering the sentence reductions obtained by the convict, contribute to the individual's progress in the resocialization process, reducing recidivism and contributing to the safety of society. Thus, the objective of this project was to automate the calculation of sentence execution, reducing human error, ensuring greater accuracy and speed in the fulfillment of sentences, through the recording of data related to the offender during their sentence, such as the number of days worked and/or studied, so that the system could automatically calculate the remission and the remaining time of the sentence, providing a more efficient management of the prison system and contributing to the resocialization of offenders.
\vspace{\onelineskip}
\noindent

\textbf{Keywords}: Criminal enforcement; prison system; automation; automatic calculation; Criminal Enforcement Law; Criminal Execution Process.
\end{otherlanguage*}
\end{resumo}
